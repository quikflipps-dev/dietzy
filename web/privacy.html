Dietzy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Dietzy is a diet, nutrition and weight-tracking app operated by JAj Group Inc., a New York corporation ("Dietzy," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Dietzy website, apps and related services (the "Service"), how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Dietzy is an educational tool. It is not a medical provider, and it is not covered by HIPAA. Because the Service handles health-related information, we treat all of it as sensitive. This policy also serves as our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws.

By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

2. Information we collect

We collect what you give us, what the Service records as you use it, and a small amount of technical data.

3. How we use information

We use your information only to run and improve Dietzy for you:

  • Provide the Service: calculate calorie, macro and nutrient targets; track weight, trends, fasting and maintenance phases; build meals; show charts, check-ins and reports.
  • Personalize safety notes: use your conditions, medications, allergies and labs to show cautions and shape AI suggestions. These are general information, not medical advice.
  • AI features: send the relevant parts of your data to an AI service to analyze meal photos and answer your questions (see section 4).
  • Accounts and billing: sign you in, manage subscriptions and give testers or friends access.
  • Support and communication: answer requests and send service messages (for example security or policy changes). Marketing emails only if you opt in, with an unsubscribe link.
  • Security and reliability: detect abuse, fix bugs and keep data safe.
  • Improve the product: look at usage patterns and de-identified, aggregated statistics.
  • Legal: comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not show third-party ads based on it.

4. AI features

Dietzy uses artificial intelligence to estimate nutrition from photos or descriptions, write daily tips and power the chat coach. AI output can be wrong; always check it, and check health decisions with a doctor.

  • Built-in AI ("AI included" plans): we send the photo or message plus the context needed to answer (for example today's food, goals and relevant health notes) to our AI provider, Anthropic, which processes it on our behalf under a contract. We choose providers that do not use our users' data to train their models.
  • Bring-your-own AI ("Plus" plan): if you connect your own AI account, your key stays on your device, and your requests go directly from your device to the provider you chose (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to that data. We do not see your key.
  • Chat history is saved to your account so the coach has context. You can clear it at any time.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only in these cases:

  • Service providers who help us run Dietzy and may use the data only for that work, under contract: hosting and database (Supabase), web hosting (Cloudflare), AI processing (section 4), email delivery, payments (Google Play, Apple, or our payment processor), and crash reporting.
  • At your direction: for example when you copy a doctor summary, export your data, or connect your own AI provider.
  • Legal reasons: to comply with a valid legal request, protect someone's safety, or enforce our Terms. Where the law allows, we will tell you first.
  • Business transfer: if Dietzy is merged, acquired or sells its assets, your information may transfer to the new owner, who must honor this policy or ask for your consent to change it.
  • De-identified data: aggregated statistics that cannot reasonably be linked to you (for example "average daily protein of users on the keto setting"). We take reasonable steps to keep it de-identified and contractually forbid anyone we share it with from re-identifying it.

We do not share consumer health data with third parties for their own purposes without your separate, express consent.

6. Selling data

We do not sell your personal information or your health data.

If we ever want to sell or license data in the future, we will:

  1. Update this policy and tell you before it takes effect.
  2. Ask for your separate, express, opt-in permission first, in a signed authorization where the law requires one (for example under Washington's My Health My Data Act). It will say what data, to whom, for what purpose, and when the permission expires.
  3. Let you say no, or revoke it later, without losing access to Dietzy.
  4. Never include anyone who has not opted in.

The de-identified, aggregated statistics described in section 5 are not "personal information" or a sale of your data.

7. Your choices and rights

You can do these yourself in the app, or by emailing quikflipps@gmail.com:

  • See and export your data (Settings, Your data).
  • Correct anything in your profile, logs or health information.
  • Delete individual entries, photos or chat history at any time, or delete your account (Settings, Your account), which erases your data from our active systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days, except what the law requires us to keep.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (for example AI features or marketing emails).
  • Choose what to share: health conditions, medications, labs and photos are optional; the core tracker works without them.

State privacy rights. Depending on where you live (including California, Washington, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and other states), you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and port your data, to opt out of sale or targeted advertising, and to limit use of sensitive data. Washington and Nevada residents have additional rights over consumer health data, including the right to know which third parties received it. To make a request, email quikflipps@gmail.com with "Privacy request" in the subject. We will verify your identity and respond within 45 days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

8. Security, retention and breaches

  • Security: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest; each account can read only its own data; access by our team is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
  • Retention: we keep your data while your account is active. If an account is inactive for 24 months, we will email you and then delete it. Photos you submit only for AI analysis are not kept after the estimate is made unless you save them.
  • Breaches: if your identifiable health information is accessed or disclosed without your authorization, we will notify you and, where required, the Federal Trade Commission and state authorities, as required by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state law.

9. Children, international users, changes and contact

  • Age: Dietzy is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete it. Parents can contact us at quikflipps@gmail.com.
  • International users: Dietzy is operated from the United States and your data is stored there. If you use it from elsewhere, you agree to that transfer.
  • Changes: we will post updates here with a new date. For material changes, especially to how we share health data, we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
  • Contact: JAj Group Inc., 444 Neptune Ave, Apt 8P, Brooklyn, NY 11224, quikflipps@gmail.com.
